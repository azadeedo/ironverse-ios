# Marketing plan: Australia first (Melbourne pilot)

Status: **plan only.** Nothing is posted, sent or paid without the owner's OK. There are no fake reviews, downloads or followers, and no spam. Paid creator posts are disclosed (#ad, AANA Code).

## Budget: A$100 a month (from launch)
| Line | A$ | Use | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Ads | 40 | Exact-match search terms: "gym workout planner", "calorie counter", "workout log", "gym tracker"; Australia only | Apple Ads dashboard: taps, installs, cost per install |
| Social video boosts | 30 | Boost the 1–2 best organic videos each month | Platform insights plus App Store Connect source |
| Creator collaboration | 20 | One micro-creator test (e.g. a free year of Pro plus a small fee) | Unique App Store offer code per creator |
| Reserve | 10 | Rollover | — |

## Channels, in order
1. TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts: the same vertical cut posted to all three.
2. App Store search (the listing is already optimised), then Apple Ads.
3. Melbourne PTs and independent gyms (partnership kit, Stage M3).
4. Referral sharing: workout and PR cards (B1, B2).

## Content pillars (4–5 videos a week)
- **Real app demos** (screen recordings): log a set, the 3D body, cuisines, the 12-week program.
- **Beginner tips:** progressive overload, rest times, how to read RIR.
- **Strength progression:** the founder's own lifts, if he's willing.
- **Food:** "calories in your Friday kebab / pho / butter chicken", using real app values.
- **Founder updates:** "building an Aussie gym app".
- **Challenges:** a 4-week consistency challenge.

**Next deliverable (Stage M1):** the full 90-day calendar. For every video it gives the hook, script, filming notes, screen recording, caption, call to action, platforms and tracking.
**Stage M3:** the gym and PT proposals, a creator message and a creator tracker.
