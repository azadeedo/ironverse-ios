Ironverse Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Applies to the Ironverse iPhone app

The short version

Ironverse has no accounts and no server for your fitness data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. We never sell your data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. The only information that ever reaches us is what you choose to send through "Suggest & report".

Data stored on your device

Your profile (name, age, height, weight, goals), workouts, sets and personal records, food and water logs, body measurements, progress photos, plans and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data, unless you have exported a backup.

Backups you create

When you choose Export backup, the app creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). Backups can be encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never receive these files.

When the app connects to the internet

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a product the app has not seen before, the barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts database to fetch nutrition information. No personal information is sent with it.
  • Optional meal-photo estimates. Only if you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and agree to the consent prompt, a meal photo you take is sent directly from your iPhone to Anthropic (Claude) to estimate its nutrition. It is sent under your own Anthropic account and Anthropic's privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Ironverse keeps no copy anywhere except on your device. Your API key is stored only on your iPhone.

Suggest & report (feedback you send)

If you use Suggest & report, the message you write, the type you pick (idea, bug, fix, other), an optional screenshot you attach, your email address if you choose to give one, and the app version and device type are sent to the developer through our form provider (Netlify Forms) and by email. We use them only to read, fix and reply. Nothing else from the app is sent. Ask us any time at the address below and we will delete your feedback.

Subscriptions

Ironverse Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has an active subscription, on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you scan a barcode or take a meal or progress photo.
  • Photos: only when you pick a photo, or save a workout card to Photos.
  • Location (while using): only while you track an outdoor run or walk. It is used to measure distance and pace, stored on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: local reminders created on your iPhone (water, workouts). No push server is involved.

Children

Ironverse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Health information

Ironverse gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
